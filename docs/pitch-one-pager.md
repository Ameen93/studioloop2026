# StudioLoop — One Platform to Run Your Gym

## The Problem

South African gym owners spend **40% of their time on admin** instead of their members. Spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and paper sign-in sheets lead to:

- **Revenue leakage** — missed and untracked payments (avg. R8,000/month lost)
- **Empty off-peak slots** — 2pm classes sitting half-empty with no way to fill them
- **No early warning** — members churn silently because there's no visibility into who's at risk
- **Staff overhead** — front desk buried in manual check-ins and payment follow-ups

International tools like Mindbody (R2,500+/month) are too expensive and too complex for independent studios.

---

## The Solution

StudioLoop replaces spreadsheet chaos with **one operating system for your gym** — affordable, built for South Africa, and designed for single-owner studios.

### What You Get

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Member Management** | Track memberships, flag at-risk members before they churn, handle sign-ups and upgrades in one place |
| **Payment Visibility** | See every transaction at a glance. Catch failed debits instantly. Automatic follow-up alerts |
| **Class Scheduling** | Build weekly timetables, manage capacity, track real attendance — desktop dashboard + mobile QR scanner |
| **Off-Peak Marketplace** | List spare capacity to consumers automatically. Fill empty slots with new paying customers |

| **Mobile Check-in** | Staff scan QR codes at the door. Works offline. No more paper lists |

## The Marketplace Advantage

StudioLoop isn't just gym software — it's a **two-sided platform**.

Your members get a free app to browse and book classes at your gym. Once they're on the app, they can discover classes at other studios too. When a consumer books at your gym through the marketplace, **you earn revenue from otherwise empty capacity** — no extra effort required.

Unlike ClassPass, StudioLoop puts **gyms first**. Better payouts, full control over which slots you list, and transparent economics.
